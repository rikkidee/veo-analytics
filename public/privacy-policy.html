Privacy Policy

Veo Analytics and the Veo Analytics Importer Chrome extension. Effective November 4, 2026.

Veo Analytics is an independent tool for coaches and team staff. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Veo. This policy explains what the extension and the web app at veo-analytics.vercel.app do with your data.

What the extension reads

The extension reads pages on app.veo.co only when you use it: while the import wizard is open in Veo Analytics, or when you click Copy This Report as JSON in its popup. It reads:

  • Team names and links from your club pages.
  • Game titles, dates, opponents and report availability from your recordings pages.
  • Team stats, player stats and key events from your match reports. Player stats can include jersey numbers and any player names or labels that Veo shows in the report.

To do this, the extension opens Veo pages in a separate window using your existing Veo sign-in, and changes which columns are shown on match reports so that it can read them.

Where that data goes

The extension passes what it reads to the Veo Analytics page open in your browser. The extension does not store this data itself or send it to any other server. The page holds it in that browser tab while you review it. When you confirm a game, the web app saves it to your account in our database, which is hosted by Supabase. Jerseys you mark as noise and games you skip are not saved as player stats.

What the extension does not collect

  • Passwords or sign-in details, including your Veo password and cookies.
  • Your browsing history, or anything from sites other than app.veo.co and Veo Analytics.
  • Analytics or advertising data. The extension contains no trackers.

What the web app stores

  • Your email address and sign-in details, to run your account.
  • The teams and rosters you enter, including player names, jersey numbers and positions.
  • The games you confirm: match details, team stats, player stats and your jersey and event assignments.

This data is only used to show you your team's statistics. Database rules limit it to the account that created it. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the service providers below.

Service providers

Supabase provides the database and sign-in. Vercel hosts the web app. Both process data on our behalf, and may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

Player information

Teams may include players under 18. Veo Analytics is meant for adults who run a team, not for use by children. Only enter or import player information that you are allowed to use for your team.

Keeping and deleting data

Saved data stays in your account until it is deleted. Removing the extension stops it reading Veo but does not delete what you already saved. To ask for your data to be deleted, use the contact below.

Chrome Web Store

Our use of information received through the extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if these practices change.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/rikkidee/veo-analytics/issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal details. We will arrange a private way to handle your request.
